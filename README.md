# WeGo - Real-Time Group Travel & Convoy Tracker

WeGo is a mobile app built for group trips, road trips, and bike rides. When you travel together with friends in different vehicles, it is easy to lose track of each other. WeGo solves this by showing everyone's live location on a single shared map.

## What WeGo Does

- **Live Group Map:** See everyone in your trip on the map in real time.
- **Easy Trip Joining:** Create a trip and share a short invite code with your friends.
- **Background Location:** Keeps tracking even when your phone screen is off or you are using another app.
- **Emergency SOS:** Tap one button to alert all group members immediately if you get a flat tire, breakdown, or need emergency help.
- **Simple & Fast:** No complicated menus. Join, start the ride, and keep everyone together.

---

## How to Use It

1. **Download & Install:** Download the APK from the link above and open it on your Android phone.
2. **Sign In or Continue as Guest:** Enter your name or log in with your account.
3. **Start or Join a Trip:**
   - **Host:** Tap "Create Tour", choose your destination, and share the 6-character code with your friends.
   - **Join:** Tap "Join Code", paste the code, and you are immediately added to the live convoy.
4. **Hit the Road:** View the live map, see distance between vehicles, and stay connected.
